# NutrientOS — HeyGen Pitch Video Script (EN)

**Goal:** a board / investor-facing pitch video for NutrientOS by OCP Nutricrops · D²nAI.
**Primary length:** ~90 sec. **Teaser variant:** ~30 sec (bottom of doc).

---

## HeyGen production setup

| Setting | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Format | 16:9 (1920×1080) for board deck / email. Also export 9:16 for mobile/LinkedIn. |
| Avatar | A confident, business-casual presenter avatar. Keep them in the **left third**; reserve the right side for the prototype screen capture. |
| Voice | English (US or UK), male or female, **"professional / confident"** tone. Speed ≈ 0.95–1.0×. |
| Pacing | ~145–150 words/min. Insert a 0.4–0.6s pause at each "—" and between scenes. |
| Background | Dark green (`#07140c`) or white to match the deck. Add the D²nAI matrix-green texture if available. |
| Captions | Burn in English subtitles (HeyGen → Captions). Keep lower-thirds for key terms. |
| B-roll | Screen-record the prototype (`index.html`) and the exec one-pager (`exec.html`); drop clips into the scenes noted below as picture-in-picture or full-frame. |

**Pronunciation notes for the voiceover:**
- *NutrientOS* → "Nutrient-O-S"
- *OCP Nutricrops* → "O-C-P Nutricrops"
- *D²nAI* → say "the **D-N-A-I** team" (don't read the "²" aloud)

## Full script — ~90 sec (6 scenes)

> Text in **VO** is what the avatar speaks, word-for-word.

### Scene 1 — Hook · 0:00–0:09
- **VO:** "In agriculture, value is moving — away from the bag of fertilizer, toward the data and the decision behind it."
- **On-screen:** NutrientOS wordmark fades in. Lower-third: *OCP Nutricrops · D²nAI*.
- **Visual:** Dark hero, matrix-green animation.

### Scene 2 — Why · 0:09–0:24
- **VO:** "Whoever owns the standard for plant nutrition will own the farmer relationship — and tomorrow's margin. So the question for OCP Nutricrops is simple: do we set that standard, or wait for someone else to?"
- **On-screen text:** "Who owns the standard owns the margin."
- **Visual:** Quick cut — soil, crops, then a data dashboard.

### Scene 3 — What · 0:24–0:50
- **VO:** "Meet NutrientOS: the open platform for plant nutrition, led by OCP Nutricrops. It works like the open ecosystems that reshaped tech — explore, contribute, build — but on an open-core model. We open the layer that creates gravity: the Nutrient Value Standard, soil datasets, reference APIs. And we keep the core that creates value: the recommendation engine, the soil digital twin, and the farmer network."
- **On-screen:** Screen capture of `index.html` → scroll the catalogue, then the **Open vs OCP Nutricrops Core** split.
- **Lower-third:** "Open the gravity. Keep the value."

### Scene 4 — Proof · 0:50–1:10
- **VO:** "And this isn't a slide — it already runs. In the Console, you pick a crop, a soil, an objective, and NutrientOS builds a personalized nutrition program in real time: powered by open assets, and by OCP Nutricrops' own engine."
- **Visual:** **Full-frame screen capture of the Console** — click crop → soil → objective, show the program + nutrient balance bars updating live.
- **On-screen text:** "Live demo, not a mockup."

### Scene 5 — How · 1:10–1:25
- **VO:** "Our plan is ninety days. The prototype is live today. Next: publish the standard on phosphate nutrition, bring on three pilot partners, and switch on the first field data — before anyone else defines the category."
- **On-screen:** 90-day timeline (reuse the `exec.html` "How" band): Weeks 1-4 ✓ · 5-8 · 9-12.

### Scene 6 — Close · 1:25–1:33
- **VO:** "NutrientOS. Own the standard. Keep the core. Lead the nutrition of a growing world."
- **On-screen:** NutrientOS logo + tagline *"Feed the soil to feed the world."* + CTA: *See the live prototype.*

**Approx. word count:** ~215 words → ~90 sec at 145 wpm.

- Keep one idea per sentence; HeyGen lip-sync is cleaner with shorter clauses.
