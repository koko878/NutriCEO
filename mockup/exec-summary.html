Decisions we need from you

Five calls to unlock the trajectory.

1 · Approve the 2025–2030 vision in three phases

Master Nutricrops information assets (2025–27) → scale to Group SBUs (2026–28) → leverage as growth driver (2028–30).

2 · Reclaim "Big Solutions" governance Sensitive

Today an entire perimeter (SAP, Salesforce, Ariba, Workday…) runs outside D²nAI without unified governance. Current Nutricrops priorities call for bringing it under our control.

3 · Structure the Cybersecurity function Explicit validation

Cyber foundations are in place (Zero Trust, NIST). What's missing is a dedicated team with formal governance: Manager + Architect + 2 SoC engineers (P0), target ISO 27001/42001 by end 2026.

4 · Approve the sourcing plan

17 P0 hires (urgent), up to 22 ETPs over 18 months. Internalisation to cut OPEX (~5.7M MAD/yr saved vs external) and build skills in-house.

5 · Sponsor Group scaling + quarterly steering

Direct CEO support to ease scaling to other SBUs, plus designation of a quarterly steering committee (CEO + CFO + CHRO + Group sponsors).
